  • Find a quiet room and hold the phone or laptop about a hand-span from your mouth.
  • Tap Record, wait a beat, say the line, then tap Stop. Pauses at the start and end get trimmed for you.
  • Big energy! The game is for kids, so lean into "SICK!" and "Wipeout!".
  • Recordings save on this device as you go, so you can stop and come back any time.

The studio needs your microphone.

Keyboard: Space = record/stop · P = play · Enter = next · ← → = move

👦 Names

Optional. The game uses the computer voice for player names, so any name works. To have your voice say a name, add it here, spelled exactly as in the game's player profile.
